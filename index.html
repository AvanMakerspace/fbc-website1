Our Vision

A creative, inclusive and empowered community where refugees — especially LGBTQ+ individuals, gender minorities and youth — use arts, culture and skills to express themselves, sustain livelihoods and live with dignity.

Our Mission

To provide safe, inclusive and transformative spaces for refugees to learn, create and produce artistic and livelihood-based work through arts education, creative production and skills training that foster expression, resilience and economic opportunity.

Type: Refugee-led, community-based arts and livelihoods collective · Status: Informal

Our objectives

  • Build artistic and creative skills among youth, students and community members.
  • Support production of films, performances, written works and crafts.
  • Create livelihood opportunities through arts and crafts training.
  • Amplify refugee and LGBTQ+ voices through storytelling and culture.
  • Promote psychosocial wellbeing and confidence through creative practice.

Decision-making & accountability

  • Strategic decisions: Executive Director with core members.
  • Program decisions: Programs Manager with program leads.
  • Financial approvals: Finance Officer with ED authorization.
  • Programs documented, reviewed and updated annually.

Safeguarding & ethics

  • Safe participation for all members and beneficiaries.
  • Zero tolerance for discrimination, abuse or exploitation.
  • Confidential handling of complaints and referrals.
